One-day working group for young researchers, on Monday 19 October 2026, opening the [Rencontre 2026 de topologie algébrique](https://topologie-2026.sciencesconf.org/) (Institut Fourier, Grenoble, 19–23 October 2026). The five talks go from presheaf categories and Cisinski's homotopy theory of presheaves to Joyal's category $$\Theta$$, $$n$$-quasi-categories, and Campbell's homotopy coherent nerve for bicategories.

## Programme

### Talk 1 — Presheaf categories I

- Basic properties of presheaf categories: the Yoneda embedding, every presheaf is a canonical colimit of representables; the example of simplicial sets.
- Universal property (cocontinuous functors out of the presheaves on a small category); the examples of topological realization and the nerve.
- The functors $$u_!$$, $$u^*$$, $$u_*$$ induced by $$u\colon A \to B$$.
- Recollections on model categories: homotopies, homotopy category, derived functors, Quillen adjunctions and equivalences; example: the Kan–Quillen model structure.

*References:* P. Gabriel and M. Zisman, *Calculus of fractions and homotopy theory*; M. Hovey, *Model categories*.

### Talk 2 — Presheaf categories II

- Cisinski's theory of $$A$$-localizers.
- Examples: the Kan–Quillen model structure; the Joyal model structure (quasi-categories); the Rezk model structure on bisimplicial sets.

*References:* D.-C. Cisinski, *Higher categories and homotopical algebra*, section 2.4; A. Joyal and M. Tierney, [*Quasi-categories vs Segal spaces*](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0607820).

### Talk 3 — The category $$\Theta$$ and $$n$$-quasi-categories

- Strict $$\omega$$-categories; the category $$\Theta$$ as a subcategory of $$\omega\text{-}\mathbf{Cat}$$; the cellular nerve.
- Definition as an iterated wreath product; horns in $$\Theta$$; the model structure for $$n$$-quasi-categories.
- Possibly, the comparison with complete $$\Theta_n$$-spaces.

*References:* D. Ara, [*Higher quasi-categories vs higher Rezk spaces*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1206.4354); A. Joyal, *Disks, duality and $$\Theta$$-categories*; C. Berger, [*Iterated wreath product of the simplex category and iterated loop spaces*](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0512575).

### Talk 4 — A homotopy coherent cellular nerve for bicategories I *(my talk)*

- Recollections on bicategories; the Duskin nerve, valued in simplicial sets.
- The homotopy coherent nerve and its full faithfulness.
- Lack's model structure on bicategories; the Quillen adjunction (Theorem 5.10).

*Reference:* A. Campbell, [*A homotopy coherent cellular nerve for bicategories*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.01999).

### Talk 5 — A homotopy coherent cellular nerve for bicategories II

- The homotopy bicategory of a 2-quasi-category; the adjunction (Theorem 6.29).
- Characterization of the equivalences of 2-quasi-categories (Theorem 7.25) and of the 2-quasi-categories that come from a bicategory (Theorem 7.28).
- The equivalence with bicategories (Theorem 8.7).

*Reference:* A. Campbell, [*A homotopy coherent cellular nerve for bicategories*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.01999).
